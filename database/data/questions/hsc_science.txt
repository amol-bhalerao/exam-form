# Rotational Dynamics | घूर्णन गतिकी
s ~ easy ~ The SI unit of moment of inertia is ~ kg m² ~ kg m ^ kg/m² ~ N m ^ kg m/s ^ J s ~ I = Σ m r², so the unit is mass × distance². ~ "I = m r²" — mass times metre times metre: kg m².
s ~ medium ~ The moment of inertia of a thin ring of mass M and radius R about its central axis perpendicular to its plane is ~ MR² ~ MR²/2 ^ 2MR²/5 ~ MR²/12 ^ 3MR²/2 ^ 2MR² ~ All the mass of a ring is at distance R from the axis, so I = MR². ~ Ring = all mass on the Rim = full MR².
s ~ medium ~ The angular momentum of a rotating body is conserved when ~ the net external torque on it is zero ~ its moment of inertia is constant ^ its angular velocity is constant ~ no force acts on it at all ^ it rotates in a vertical plane ^ its kinetic energy is constant ~ dL/dt = τ, so L is constant when the net external torque is zero. ~ Spinning dancer pulls her arms in — no outside twist (torque) — so L stays the same and she spins faster.
tf ~ easy ~ A ballet dancer spins faster when she pulls her arms in because her moment of inertia decreases. ~ True ~ ~ L = Iω stays constant; smaller I means larger ω. ~ Arms in → I down → spin up.
f ~ medium ~ The minimum speed at the highest point of a vertical circle of radius r for a body to complete the circle is √(____). ~ rg ^ gr ~ ~ At the top, tension can be zero and gravity alone provides the centripetal force: mv²/r = mg, so v = √(rg). ~ Top of the loop: "just gravity" → v² = rg.
# Mechanical Properties of Fluids | द्रवांचे यांत्रिक गुणधर्म
s ~ easy ~ The SI unit of surface tension is ~ N/m ~ N m ^ N/m² ~ J m ^ Pa ^ kg/m ~ Surface tension is force per unit length. ~ Tension along a line: Newton per metre.
s ~ medium ~ The rise of liquid in a capillary tube is ~ inversely proportional to the radius of the tube ~ directly proportional to the radius of the tube ^ independent of the radius ~ proportional to the square of the radius ^ proportional to the density of the liquid ^ proportional to g ~ h = 2T cosθ / (r ρ g), so h ∝ 1/r. ~ Thinner tube, higher climb.
tf ~ easy ~ Bernoulli's principle is a statement of the conservation of energy for a flowing fluid. ~ True ~ ~ P + ½ρv² + ρgh = constant along a streamline — total energy per unit volume is conserved. ~ Bernoulli = Budget of energy.
s ~ hard ~ A liquid with angle of contact greater than 90° in a glass capillary will ~ fall below the outside level ~ rise above the outside level ^ stay at the same level ~ rise and then overflow ^ freeze at the meniscus ^ form a flat meniscus ~ For θ > 90° (like mercury in glass), cos θ is negative, so h is negative — the liquid is depressed. ~ Mercury "hates" glass — it goes down.
m ~ medium ~ Which of the following increase the terminal velocity of a small sphere falling through a viscous liquid? ~ Larger radius of the sphere ^ Greater density of the sphere ~ Greater viscosity of the liquid ^ Greater density of the liquid ^ Smaller radius of the sphere ~ v = 2r²(ρ − σ)g / 9η, so v rises with r² and with sphere density, and falls with viscosity and liquid density. ~ "2-r-squared over 9-eta": big, heavy balls fall faster; thick (viscous) liquids slow them.
# Thermodynamics | ऊष्मागतिकी
s ~ easy ~ In an isothermal process, which quantity stays constant? ~ Temperature ~ Pressure ^ Volume ~ Heat ^ Entropy ^ Internal energy of a real gas ~ "Iso-thermal" means same temperature. ~ Iso = same, therm = heat/temperature.
s ~ medium ~ In an adiabatic process ~ no heat is exchanged with the surroundings ~ temperature remains constant ^ pressure remains constant ~ volume remains constant ^ internal energy remains constant ^ work done is zero ~ Adiabatic: Q = 0, so ΔU = −W. ~ "A-dia-batic" — heat cannot pass through.
tf ~ medium ~ According to the first law of thermodynamics, heat supplied to a system is equal to the increase in internal energy plus the work done by the system. ~ True ~ ~ Q = ΔU + W. ~ Money in (Q) = savings (ΔU) + spending (W).
f ~ hard ~ The efficiency of a Carnot engine working between 500 K and 300 K is ____ %. ~ 40 ^ 40% ~ ~ η = 1 − T₂/T₁ = 1 − 300/500 = 0.4 = 40%. ~ Carnot: "one minus cold over hot" — always kelvin.
# Current Electricity | विद्युत धारा
s ~ easy ~ Kirchhoff's current law is based on the conservation of ~ charge ~ energy ^ momentum ~ mass ^ current density ^ power ~ Charge cannot pile up at a junction, so current in = current out. ~ Junction law = "what goes in must come out" (charge).
s ~ medium ~ A potentiometer is preferred over a voltmeter for measuring emf because ~ it draws no current from the cell at the null point ~ it has a very low resistance ^ it is cheaper ~ its wire is made of copper ^ it measures current directly ^ it works only with AC ~ At balance no current flows from the cell, so the full emf is measured. ~ Null point = no current = true emf.
s ~ medium ~ The Wheatstone bridge is balanced when ~ P/Q = R/S ~ P + Q = R + S ^ PQ = RS + 1 ~ P/R = S/Q ^ P − Q = R − S ^ all four resistances are equal only ~ At balance no current flows through the galvanometer and P/Q = R/S. ~ "P over Q equals R over S" — alphabet in order.
f ~ medium ~ To convert a galvanometer into an ammeter, a low resistance called a ____ is connected in parallel. ~ shunt ~ ~ The shunt bypasses most of the current, protecting the galvanometer. ~ Ammeter → shunt in parallel (A–P); Voltmeter → high resistance in series (V–S).
