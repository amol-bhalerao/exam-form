# Grammar — Voice and Speech | व्याकरण
s ~ easy ~ Choose the passive voice: "She writes a letter." ~ A letter is written by her. ~ A letter was written by her. ^ A letter has been written by her. ^ A letter is being written by her. ^ A letter will be written by her. ^ She is written a letter. ~ Simple present active → "is/am/are + past participle" in passive. ~ Keep the tense: "writes" (present) → "is written".
s ~ medium ~ Choose the indirect speech: He said, "I am tired." ~ He said that he was tired. ~ He said that I am tired. ^ He said that he is tired. ^ He says that he was tired. ^ He told that he had been tired. ^ He said he will be tired. ~ Reporting verb in past → present tense becomes past; "I" becomes "he". ~ Past reporting verb pushes the tense one step back.
f ~ easy ~ Fill in the blank with the correct article: He is ____ honest man. ~ an ~ ~ "Honest" begins with a vowel sound (the h is silent). ~ Use "an" before a vowel SOUND, not just a vowel letter.
tf ~ medium ~ "Neither of the boys was present" is grammatically correct. ~ True ~ ~ "Neither" is singular, so it takes "was". ~ Neither = not this ONE, not that ONE → singular.
